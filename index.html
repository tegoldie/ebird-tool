Sydney Bird Finder points you to the best places to go birding.

  • See the best hotspots. Every eBird hotspot within driving distance of Sydney is ordered by how many species you can expect to see in a visit.
  • Find birds you haven’t seen yet. Rare Birds Mode screens out the birds you’ll see everywhere and shows you the species that make each hotspot special.
  • Know when to go. Monthly bar charts show how birds come and go with the seasons.
  • Chase your target bird. Use Choose Species to see the best hotspots for a single species.

The tool uses checklists submitted to eBird. This data can be patchy and skewed, so the tool adjusts for who made each checklist and when.
